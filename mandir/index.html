Privacy Policy - Mandir

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Mandir (Android package com.chalisa.app) is a devotional reading and listening app published by PrepMitra.

Data stored on your device

The app stores preferences and devotional activity locally, including favourites, reading and listening positions, language and text-size preferences, reminders, manually selected city, puja and vrat selections, private prayer intentions, offline audio, and custom verse timings.

No account is required. The current app has no advertising, analytics, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. The developer does not operate a server that receives your prayer intentions or devotional activity.

Audio and external services

When streaming or downloading is available and you choose to use it, the app connects directly to the recording's external host. That host receives normal network request information, including your IP address and the requested resource. Internet Archive is an audio host referenced by the current app. External hosts handle requests under their own privacy policies.

Bundled devotional texts and images can be used offline. Bundled audio, where included, can also be played offline. Streaming and new downloads require connectivity. This policy describes data handling and does not grant rights to any third-party recording.

Permissions and reminders

Network permissions support audio streaming and downloads. Notification permission supports reminders and playback notifications. Foreground media playback and wake-lock permissions support background playback. Boot-completed permission supports rescheduling local background work after restart.

The app does not request GPS location permission. The city preference is selected manually.

Android backup and transfer

Android backup and device-transfer services can include data allowed by the app's backup rules, including its database. These services depend on your device and account settings. The developer does not receive these backups; your device provider's terms and privacy policy apply.

Deletion

To remove locally stored app data, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall it. Backups may be retained separately; manage them through your device or account backup settings.

Support and changes

If you email support, your email provider processes your message and contact details. Information you send is used to respond to your request. Please do not send passwords or private prayer intentions.
